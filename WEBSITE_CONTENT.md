## 4. How We Work — `/how-we-work`
**SEO title:** How We Work | Juanovio International Trading
**H1:** A Clearer Path from Inquiry to Opportunity.
**Intro:** Every trade begins with a conversation. We seek to understand the commercial requirement, identify potential alignment, and coordinate a practical path for further evaluation.
**Steps:**
1. **Tell us what you need.** Buyers describe product, specifications, estimated volume, destination, and timing. Suppliers describe product, origin, capacity, and available documentation.
2. **Evaluate the opportunity.** Review commercial fit, potential sourcing options, and key transaction constraints.
3. **Align the fundamentals.** Clarify proposed commercial roles, specifications, logistics responsibilities, documentation, payment approach, and applicable terms.
4. **Coordinate next steps.** When both sides see a viable opportunity, continue discussions toward an agreed transaction structure.
**Disclosure:** Commercial engagement, counterparties, product availability, regulatory requirements, and final terms are evaluated case by case. No shipment, sourcing, or financial outcome is guaranteed.
**CTA:** Discuss Your Requirements → /contact
